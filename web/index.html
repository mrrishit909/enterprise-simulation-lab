Enterprise Simulation Lab

An invented subscription business as agents: customer cohorts, prospects, a competitor and a retention desk, each calibrated from 36 months of history. A 12% price rise and a competitor discount through 10,000 seeded scenarios, an outlier replayed bit for bit, the inputs that matter, and a decision with its evidence, every number set against the simulation truth. …
The business, its customers, its history, the competitor and the true behaviour behind them are generated by a simulator in this repository; nothing is a real company or real customer data. The truth is shown only to judge the calibrated agents, which never see it. No language model or outside service is called.
